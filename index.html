How to Add to HotPlayer

  1. Open HotPlayer on your Smart TV, Android TV, or mobile device.
  2. Visit the HotPlayer management portal (e.g. hotplayer.app) or go to Settings → Add Playlist in the app.
  3. Enter your MAC address/Device Key if prompted.
  4. Select Playlist Type: M3U URL.
  5. Paste the M3U Playlist URL copied above into the URL field.
  6. Click Upload / Save and reload the HotPlayer app.
